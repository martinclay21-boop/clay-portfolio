## How he works (design process, in his words)

1. **Find the root need.** Get clear on what is actually being asked, and the context it lives in. Miss this and everything after is off.
2. **Gather the pieces.** Look everywhere for inspiration and dig into real user needs. There is no single right answer, so collect a lot before committing.
3. **Interpret & build.** Turn it into wireframes and flows that do exactly what was asked, and maybe a little more.
4. **Test, and let it fail.** Put it in front of real people expecting it to break. Every failure points to the next fix.
5. **Sweat the details.** The small stuff: a color a shade off, a button a few pixels over. Nothing is ever perfect, but it gets close.

**Design beliefs:**
- Start with the root need. A design out of context is just decoration.
- There is no single right answer, only the best interpretation of the problem.
- Iteration over perfection. Failure is where the learning is.
- Purpose first, polish last. Backed by research, but it should still look good and feel effortless.
